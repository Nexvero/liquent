# LQ-2533 Phase-output capture-stability contract

- Successful capture requires stable child and namespace facts over two passes.
- The opened child descriptor remains the anchor for both child measurements.
- The visible fixed name is independently measured twice from the workspace.
- Every observation must retain private type, mode, owner, device, and inode.
- Terminal workspace metadata closes the complete capture observation window.
- Stability evidence grants no publication or deployment authority.
