# LQ-2429 Cross-phase output-continuity gate

- All captured child identities are checked before and after every later phase.
- The four-identity set must be complete after the final bundle phase.
- Terminal workspace inventory compares every directory to its retained identity.
- Controlled evidence creation cannot replace any previously accepted child.
- The final relative workspace rename follows this terminal continuity check.
- A same-named, same-mode replacement therefore cannot reach publication.
