# LQ-2547 Post-child-pass entry rejection

- A root entry created after terminal retained-child metadata is still rejected.
- The entry cannot influence or become part of the expected identity mapping.
- Its type, mode, owner, content, and name do not grant acceptance.
- Rejection occurs before terminal root success or receipt parsing.
- No later phase can legitimize the late intermediate entry.
- Temporary workspace cleanup remains the bounded local consequence.
