# LQ-2399 Roundtrip-wheel inventory contract

- `sdist-wheel-roundtrip` contains exactly one rebuilt wheel and no other entry.
- Its expected name and SHA-256 digest are retained from the successful sdist phase.
- The digest must equal the original bound distribution wheel digest.
- Extra, missing, stale, temporary, or linked entries fail closed.
- Roundtrip evidence grants no publication or promotion authority.
