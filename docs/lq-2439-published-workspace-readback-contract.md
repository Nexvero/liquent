# LQ-2439 Published-workspace readback contract

- Publication success requires verification through the final public output name.
- Root and child identity checks alone cannot substitute for evidence readback.
- The exact controlled evidence bytes and complete workspace inventory are mandatory.
- Readback occurs inside the still rollback-capable publication operation.
- Any mismatch or technical failure remains detail-limited and fails closed.
- This local readback grants no deployment or external publication authority.
