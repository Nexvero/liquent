# LQ-2491 Immediate precommit-namespace contract

- Publication revalidates its namespace after all potentially lengthy child checks.
- Early target absence cannot substitute for an immediate pre-rename check.
- Parent, source workspace, and destination name are measured through one descriptor.
- A newly occupied destination causes rejection without overwrite or removal.
- Source and destination remain fixed relative names under the bound private parent.
- This local commit discipline grants no deployment or external publication authority.
