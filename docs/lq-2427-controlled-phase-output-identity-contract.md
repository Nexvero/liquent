# LQ-2427 Controlled phase-output identity contract

- Four fixed phases create the four fixed private workspace directories.
- The controller binds each child device and inode immediately after its phase.
- A path, mode, owner, or content check cannot substitute for child identity.
- Every retained identity remains mandatory through final workspace publication.
- Replacement, deletion and recreation, or symbolic redirection fail closed.
- These identities are local custody facts and grant no publication authority.
