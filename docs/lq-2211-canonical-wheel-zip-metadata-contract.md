# LQ-2211 Canonical wheel ZIP metadata contract

- All wheel members share one canonical ZIP timestamp.
- Unix is the sole creator system and ZIP 2.0 is the sole format level.
- Archive and member comments are absent.
- Member extra fields are absent.
- Reserved, volume, and internal attributes are zero.
- Metadata cannot carry hidden identity or alternate extraction behavior.
- The contract adds no installation or publication authority.
